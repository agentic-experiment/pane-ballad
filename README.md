# pane-ballad

A stained-glass rhythm game where you catch falling light to illuminate cathedral window panes and weave a modal musical ballad.

### Mechanics & Design

* **The Cathedral Window**: Four vertical gothic window lanes receive cascading motes of colored light.
* **Rhythm & Lane Striking**: Press `D`, `F`, `J`, `K` (or arrow keys, `1`-`4`, or tap/click directly on lanes) as falling light motes cross the windowsill threshold.
* **Illumination & Sound**: Catching notes on the beat illuminates stained-glass glass panels and triggers harmonic modal chords synthesized live via Web Audio.
* **Radiance & Movements**: Consecutive catches build your radiance bar and advance the ballad through progressive musical movements. Missed notes dim the glass.
